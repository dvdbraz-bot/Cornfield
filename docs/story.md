STAY AWAY FROM THE CORNFIELD 

~PROLOGUE~

The farmhouse stood alone. A solitary figure surrounded by a cornfield like a mass of ants ready to descend on a piece of discarded meat. Justin, a boy of 10, and his younger brother, Phillip, sat at the kitchen table playing their third game of Snakes and Ladders. Their grandfather, to whom the house belonged, sat in the lounge mindlessly watching the TV. He fought off sleep as his eye lids struggled in their dominance to close and send him off to the world of sleep. 

A cut-off-low recently swept through the region like a reaper's scythe, bringing an abundance of rain.  It had just stopped raining when two children aged about ten came running out of their house. Laughing and shouting, they ran into the cornfield at the back of their house. Their father, an elderly man, came out of the house chasing after them. 
"Boys!" He shouted angrily. "Get yourselves back in the house now! Don't make me come in their after you!" 
He waited for five minutes, and then he ran into the cornfield. 
"Bob, James! Where are you?" He shouted. "When I find you there's going to be hell!"
He started to swear aloud when something hard hit him on the back.
"Now they're throwing rocks at me." He said aloud.
He turned around to see what had hit him. He picked up the object and screamed. He was holding a head, his son's head. It had a frightened expression frozen on its face. Its eyes were glazed and it had a pentagram carved in its forehead. Blood was gushing out from where the neck should have been. Blood also trickled from the carving in the forehead. He was in such a shock that he never heard anything coming up from behind him. He only realized it was there when he felt the immense pain of something very powerful digging into his back and grabbing his heart.

  
~CHAPTER 1~

It was a hot cloudless day. It was an extremely quiet day. The only noise that was heard was an engine of a red porche. It travelled up a dirt road for five minutes before turning into a concrete driveway.
The driveway was old and riddled with cracks. The driveway continued for a few metres and stopped in front of a large white house. The front of the house had a large window with green curtains on the inside. The door was brown and had a large brass knob on the front.
The porche came to a halt, the engine stopped. The door opened and a tall person stepped out of the car. He had dark hair and brown eyes. He wore blue jeans, a pair of Nike shoes and a tight plain white t-shirt. As he started to make his way to the boot of the car, the front door of the house burst open and a young woman with blonde shoulder length hair came running out.
"Michael!" She shouted as she ran towards him. She had light blue eyes and she was wearing a red skirt. Her hair bobbed up and down as she ran to him. She wrapped her thin, white arms around his tanned, muscular body. He put his huge arms around her tiny waist.
"It's been so long." She said to him.
His name was Michael Cole and he had come to visit his father and sister on their small farm in Arizona for a vacation. He was a horror writer and lived in New York City.
His sister's name was Jenny and the last time he came for a visit was two years earlier.
Michael started walking up to the house when an old man stepped out of the front doorway. He had fine grey hair and blue eyes. He wore a blue checkered shirt, brown pants and brown leather shoes. Michael ran up the stares and hugged his father. They all went inside to catch up on old times.
Michael found himself in the living room. It was painted light-blue and had a navy-blue leather lounge suit. It had brown carpets and an old Phillips T.V set on a white wall unit.
After chatting with his father and sister, Michael got up and went to go fetch the luggage from his car.
"Oh yes," Michael said as he got up off the couch. "I saw a cop car passing me as I came up the dirt road, why were they here?"
Jenny looked at her father and then stared back at Michael.
"Remember Mr. Jacobs." She said.
" Yes."
"Well, him and his two grandchildren were murdered last night."

~CHAPTER 2~


"M-murdered." Michael stammered. "How?"
They were decapitated, their hearts were ripped out from behind and they had satanic drawings carved into their foreheads." His sister answered.

She paused for a moment before carrying on. "The police think there might be Satanists hiding in our cornfield."
Michael was stunned. He couldn't believe that on of his father's best friends was murdered. Mr. Jacobs lived on the opposite side of the Cole's cornfield. He owned a small piece of land and shared Mr. Cole's cornfield. Him and Mr. Cole were friends for as long as Michael could remember.
Remember that letter I wrote to you about my new boyfriend, Jason." Jenny said changing the subject.
"Yes." Michael said. 
"Well him and his sister are coming to stay for a week over here."
"When are they coming?" Michael asked.
"They should be here soon." Jenny answered.
"Don't you think you should start unpacking?" Mr Cole asked. Michael nodded and headed towards the car. He pulled out two large dark blue suitcases and one briefcase. The two suitcases had four wheels each, as this made them easier to carry. Michael took the suitcases and gave the briefcase to his sister. He motioned her to follow him up to his room. 
He stepped into his old room to find his old bed that hadn't been slept in for nearly two years. His room was painted dark green and the cupboard doors were painted white.
When he had finished unpacking, he took the briefcase down to the kitchen table and opened it up to reveal his laptop computer. He had already got inspiration to write his new novel. He felt guilty knowing that the novel would be based on Mr. Jacob's murder, but who had to know that?
As Michael reached the end of the first chapter, the sound of a beeping car was heard from outside.



~CHAPTER 3~

Michael got up and started walking to the front door, but Jenny beat him to it. She swung the door open and ran outside.
Michael stepped onto the front porch and saw an old, white Volkswagen Beetle come onto the driveway. The Beetle parked behind the Porsche. The door opened and a tall person climbed out of the tiny car. Michael was amazed. He was surprised that someone that tall could fit in a car that tiny. Jenny ran and hugged her boyfriend Jason. He looked kind of strange with a slight tan, white socks and "Reebok" sneakers.
The passenger door opened and a pail looking woman appeared. She had thick brown hair and beady looking eyes. She was wearing s Backstreetboys T-shirt.
Michael wanted to vomit. He hated the Backstreetboys as much as a bacterium hates antibiotics.
The woman also wore Levis jeans and Fila sneakers.
These people are a fashion statement. Michael thought to himself.
"Michael," Jenny said. "This is my boyfriend Jason Dannon and that's his sister Jean." 
Jean gave Michael a shy smile and then looked away. Michael and Jason shook hands.
"Man!" Jason said. "Jenny said you were well built but I didn't expect an Arnold Schwarzenegger."
"Well I work out quite a lot." Michael said. He really didn't know what to say. 
Jason and Jean went upstairs to unpack. After they had unpacked, they came down for lunch. For lunch they just had scrambled egg.
Jenny was busy washing up when an old Labrador came strolling into the kitchen. Jenny gave a startled cry, then she said playfully: "Spike! You old dog, what do you think you're doing scaring me like that? I suppose you want a snack." The dog gave a bark as if saying yes. Jenny went to the fridge, took out some meat chunks and held out her hand. Spike took the chunks gracefully from her hand. Spike lifted his head and looked in the direction of the cornfield, he gave two threatening barks. He then ran full speed out the kitchen and into the cornfield.
Jenny ran into the living room. "I think Spike's onto something!" She said excitedly.
They all got up and walked faster than normal towards the cornfield. As they got to the border of the field, they heard a dog yelp in pain.


~CHAPTER 4~


Jason and Michael both ran into the cornfield. They shouted Spike's name but got no response. The yellow ears of corn gave an eerie glow in the sunlight. As they approached the centre of the field, a black figure could be seen a few metres away. Both men gasped in horror when the figure became recognisable. There, lying in front of them was Spike. His head was lying a few yards away from his body. His fur was sticky with dark, red blood. Blood was oozing out of his severed neck. They were staring at the dog's body in such a shock that they didn't hear Jenny come up from behind them. They didn't even hear her scream. 
"Who did this?" She whispered loudly. No one replied. 
Later in the evening Spike was buried. "May he rest in peace." Mr. Cole said.
More like may he rest in pieces. Michael thought. He scolded himself for thinking that.

After dinner everyone went to bed. Jason lay awake in his bed. He lay on his back staring at the ceiling. He was about to drift off to sleep when he heard a barking form outside. He looked out the window to see a dog looking exactly like Spike. He put a plain white shirt over his tartan boxers, ran out of the house and straight onto the cornfield. "Spike!" He whispered loudly. As he approached the centre of the field, something came running at top speed towards him. He felt an immense pain as something very powerful dug into his back and grabbing his heart. 
He turned around to face a large creature covered in black fur. It had two slanted red eyes, razor sharp teeth and two huge horns protruding from its forehead, which curled towards the top. It stood on two legs and had hooves instead of feet. In one hand it held Jason's still beating heart. Jason was amazed, shocked and terrified. He was amazed that he was still alive. The creature roared at Jason, it lifted its free hand and a giant, sharp hook emerged out of its palm. 
It grabbed Jason and spun him around so his back faced the beast. 
Blood gushed out of his back.
It placed the hook around his neck, and with a swift pull, his head was off.

It was three fifteen and Jason wasn't back yet. Jenny decided to go look for him.
As she walked into the cornfield, she had a funny feeling that something was watching her. As she got near to the middle of the field, something hit her in the back of her head. She turned round in terror to see a bird fly off.
"Just a bird." She said aloud.
It was six in the evening and Jason still hadn't returned.
"They've got him." Jenny said."
"Who's got him?" Michael asked.
"The Satanists. I know they do. Jenny looked out of the kitchen window one last time.
No Jason. With a tear in her eye, she went up to bed.

Jenny's eyes opened with fear. She jumped out of bed and ran to her window. She stared in horror as she looked outside. Standing in the garden was Jason. He was holding his severed head in his right hand and his heart in his left. The head looked up at Jenny. Slowly, its mouth opened. 
"Jenny," It whispered. "Come here. I need you, I'm scared. Please, help me. 
She ran out of her bedroom, through the hallway, down the stairs and out the front door straight into the cornfield.
"Jason!" She shouted aloud.

She felt something touch her shoulder. She froze.

Jenny spun round to see Jason standing in front of her. His head was back on his body. 
Jenny ran to Jason and hugged him. 
"I was sure they had you." Jenny said. "I was so scared."
Jason pulled away and smiled at her. His eyes turned red. His teeth grew long and sharp. He turned her round. Jenny couldn't move. Something was controlling her urge to panic. 
He dug his hand deep into her back and grabbed her heart. Then he let her go.
Jenny stared in terror at her heart. It was still beating. 
Thick black hair sprouted everywhere from Jason's body. He grew larger, his skin stretched so much that it tore off his body. 
Jenny was highly confused and scared. She didn't know what was going on. 
Her still beating heart was in the creature's right hand, a large sharp hook emerged out of its left. Jenny regained the ability to run. She turned round and sprinted towards the house. The beast squeezed her heart, Jenny felt the pain. It was so immense that she fell to her knees. 
The creature put the hook around Jenny's neck and sliced he head off. Her head fell to the ground with a sickening thud. The beast picked up the head and with its claw, carved a pentagram in the head. It faced the body. Blood was streaming out of the severed neck. With the heart and head in one hand, it grabbed the corpse by the feet and dragged it to the centre of the field.
